# Synthetic corpus notice

Every payload under `raw/` was authored specifically for ULPF testing. The corpus contains no captured customer traffic, personal data, credentials, proprietary vendor samples, or claims of compatibility with a real product.

Names such as `Example`, `Synthetic Firewall`, and `Lab IDS` are fictional. IP addresses use documentation ranges from RFC 5737 or private address space. Domain names use the reserved `.example` or `.invalid` suffixes.
